5. WHAT IS OUR 3D SPATIAL AUDIO & HAPTICS ARCHITECTURE? (CO4)

Our multi-sensory gaming architecture directs player attention toward off-screen AR targets using spatialized acoustic and tactile biofeedback sub-systems:

  • Head-Related Transfer Functions (HRTF): Synthesizes microsecond Interaural Time Difference (ITD ≤ 0.7 ms) and Interaural Level Difference (ILD) based on anatomical head, torso, and pinna scattering.
  • Acoustic Head-Shadow Low-Pass Filter: When an AR target is behind the player (|θ| > 90°), pinna and cranial diffraction dynamically attenuate high frequencies down to 2,500 Hz: f_cutoff(θ) = Lerp(22000 Hz, 2500 Hz, (|θ| / 180°)^1.5)
  • Inverse Distance 3D Attenuation Curve: Perceived loudness decays following physical acoustic laws: Gain(d) = d_min / (d_min + α × (d - d_min))    [d_min = 1.0m, d_max = 25.0m, α = 1.2]
  • Dynamic Audio Beaconing: Accelerates ping recurrence intervals from 1.2s down to 0.3s as the player rotates toward alignment.
